# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS

## Users

Parents, caregivers, and family members purchasing baby supplies. They are frequently time-constrained, shopping on mobile devices while caring for infants, and need immediate clarity on sizes, brand trust, and pricing.

## Product Purpose

An online baby store offering everything needed for infants and young children—clothes, care and cleaning products, and essentials. The initial launch catalog focuses specifically on diapers, with an architecture ready to expand into full baby departments.

## Positioning

A dedicated, joyful baby specialist store with trusted local and international brands, clear stage/size categorization (T0–T5), and upfront pricing, designed specifically around parental convenience.

## Operating Context

Primarily mobile and responsive web browsing. Parents often shop during brief windows between baby care duties, requiring quick search/filter by diaper size, package count, and price, with seamless checkout or order placement.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Catalog initial phase: Diaper selection across major brands (Babysec, Pequeñín, Winny) spanning sizes T0 to T5, package counts (16u to 100u), and prices in COP.
- Planned future categories: Baby clothing, personal hygiene & care, nursery essentials, and accessories.
- Technical stack: Vite + React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
- Asset note: Current product photography in `assets/` is in HEIC format; web delivery will require web-compatible formats (PNG/WebP/JPG).

## Brand Commitments

- Aesthetic direction: Children-themed with vibrant, cheerful colors suited for babies and early childhood.
- Tone and voice: Warm, trustworthy, playful, and empathetic to parents.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product photography and inventory metadata in `assets/` covering:
  - Babysec Ultra Protect (T1 30u, T2 30u, T4 30u, T5 30u)
  - Pequeñín (Dumbo-pack T1 100u, Extra Protección T1 & T2 50u, New Baby T0 50u)
  - Winny (Sensitive T0 50u, T1 30u & 50u; Ultratrim Sec T1 16u, T2 30u & 50u)
- Real pricing and packaging metrics in Colombian Pesos (COP).

## Product Principles

1. **Effortless Scannability**: Present sizes (T0–T5), quantities, and prices clearly so busy parents can choose without hesitation.
2. **Joyful Childlike Energy with Reassuring Trust**: Combine vibrant, playful colors with clean, dependable product information and hygiene standards.
3. **Extensible Catalog Foundation**: Build product structures and layouts to effortlessly accommodate future categories (clothing, bath, feeding) beyond diapers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- High contrast text over vibrant accents to ensure readability in varying lighting conditions.
- Generous touch targets for comfortable one-handed mobile navigation.
