# Surface Brief: Storefront

## Scope & Visitor Mode
- Target: storefront
- Mode: Persuade

## Direction contract

THESIS: Baby Store refuses the sterile, cold medical warehouse aesthetic typical of pharmacies and bulk diaper distributors. It establishes a vibrant, tactile children's playroom environment that sparks joy while making diaper sizing and Colombian Peso pricing instantly glanceable for busy parents.

OWN-WORLD: "The Playroom Block System" with vibrant sunshine yellow, sky blue, coral strawberry, and mint sprout accents resting on warm cloud-white backgrounds. Chunky child-safe rounded blocks, tactile pills for diaper stages (T0–T5), and bouncy spring feedback.

STORY: Parents immediately see their trusted diaper brands (Winny, Pequeñín, Babysec) organized by size and stage, filter by baby weight/stage with a single thumb tap, compare package counts and unit prices, and place an order seamlessly.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Joyful header with brand badge and shopping cart trigger; a lively hero banner announcing newborn to toddler essentials; horizontal scrolling pills for diaper stages (T0, T1, T2, T4, T5) and brands; leading into the first row of tactile diaper product cards with package counts, clear COP prices, and one-tap order actions.

FORM: The Playroom Block System, Candidate 1, seed key fc08ce30.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
